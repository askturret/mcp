# Mutation-audit inventory (#428 stage 1)

Generated by `.github/scripts/check-mutation-audit.mjs`. **Report-only.**
A site is WITNESSED when neutralising it turns its guard's self-test red —
witnessed *relative to what that self-test exercises*, which is the honest limit.

## Read the unwitnessed count with two caveats

**1. Some part of it may be MASKING rather than absence.** The unknown-route
detector only examines assertions tied to sites already found WITNESSED, so a
failure route outside the enumeration can make a genuinely-witnessed site
record as unwitnessed while never tripping the detector — QA demonstrated
exactly that with a `process.exitCode = 1` route, where a site flipped to
witnessed once the hidden route was removed. So the unwitnessed figure is an
upper bound on genuine absence, not a precise count of it.

**2. It is environment-dependent (#429).** These numbers require a working
`PATH`. This repo's agent environment has shipped a space-separated `PATH`, on
which `env: node` fails in child processes — several guards then go
`cannot check`, and the totals move. #429 has bitten three agents; it is the
precondition for reproducing anything below. Re-run with a sane `PATH` before
treating a difference as a real change.

- measured guards: **26**
- failure sites: **172**
- witnessed: **124**
- unwitnessed: **48**
- unreachable (no self-test, #431): **0** sites across 0 scripts
- cannot check (non-green baseline): **0** scripts
- cannot check, as sites: **0**
- exemptions on the ledger (#532): **0** — 48 unwitnessed site(s) carry no entry

`witnessed + unwitnessed + cannot-check sites = failure sites`. The site-level
figure is what closes that identity, and it is what makes a fall in `witnessed`
legible as a change of CATEGORY rather than a loss of coverage (#438).
