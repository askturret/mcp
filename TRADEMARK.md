# AskTurret Trademark Policy

AskTurret owns the "AskTurret" name, logo, and related marks to protect the project's identity and prevent consumer confusion. This policy explains how the AskTurret name and logo can be used.

**The short version:** You can use "AskTurret" in truthful, descriptive statements and community activities. You cannot use it to name a product or service that might be confused with the official AskTurret offering.

---

## Permitted Uses

The following uses are **always permitted** without asking:

### 1. Truthful Descriptive Statements

You may use AskTurret in truthful, non-endorsing descriptions, such as:

- *"Built with AskTurret MCP"*
- *"Powered by AskTurret"*
- *"AskTurret-compatible adapter"*
- *"An OpenAPI-to-MCP solution like AskTurret"*
- *"A fork of AskTurret"*
- *"Based on AskTurret MCP"*

These statements accurately describe a factual relationship between your work and AskTurret.

### 2. Community Activities

Community members are welcome to:

- **Tutorials and blog posts:** Write about AskTurret, create video tutorials, publish guides
- **Meetups and conferences:** Organize or speak at AskTurret-focused meetups ("AskTurret Community Meetup")
- **Open-source contributions:** Create adapters, plugins, and extensions for AskTurret
- **Research and academic work:** Reference AskTurret in papers or case studies

Examples:
- *"AskTurret MCP Community Meetup (Boston)"*
- *"Building an Express Adapter for AskTurret"*
- *"Integrating AskTurret with Kubernetes"*

### 3. Nominative (Comparative) Use

You may use "AskTurret" to compare your product to ours:

- *"Unlike AskTurret, our tool generates code instead of using a runtime"*
- *"AskTurret MCP vs. our solution: a comparison"*
- *"We improved upon AskTurret's approach to policy"*

The key requirement: the comparison must be **accurate and fair**. Do not make false claims about AskTurret.

### 4. Framework Adapter Naming

If you create an adapter for AskTurret, you may name it clearly:

- *"AskTurret Koa Adapter"* ✅
- *"Koa support for AskTurret MCP"* ✅
- *"My Koa AskTurret extension"* ✅

You should also include a clear disclaimer: *"This is an unofficial AskTurret community adapter, not maintained by the AskTurret team."*

## Reserved Uses

The following uses are **reserved** and require permission:

### 1. Official Product Names

You **cannot** use "AskTurret" in the name of a product or service without explicit permission, including:

- *"AskTurret Cloud"* ❌
- *"AskTurret Enterprise"* ❌
- *"AskTurret SaaS"* ❌
- *"AskTurret Pro"* ❌
- *"AskTurret Managed Service"* ❌

These names could be confused with official AskTurret offerings or create a false impression of official endorsement.

### 2. Certification Marks

The phrase **"AskTurret MCP Compatible"** and any related certification marks are reserved. Only officially approved products may use these marks.

If you believe your product or adapter deserves certification, contact [trademark@askturret.com](mailto:trademark@askturret.com) to discuss formal certification.

### 3. Hosted Services

Hosted services (SaaS, managed services, cloud offerings) that could be mistaken for an official AskTurret service require permission:

- *"AskTurret Cloud Platform"* ❌
- *"Hosted AskTurret"* ❌
- *"AskTurret as a Service"* ❌
- *"askturret.io"* or similar domain ❌

**What's allowed:** Describing what you built using AskTurret, e.g., *"We built our API platform using AskTurret MCP."*

### 4. Modified Distributions

You **cannot** redistribute AskTurret (modified or unmodified) under a name that could imply official endorsement or create confusion:

- A fork branded as *"AskTurret Enterprise Edition"* ❌
- A heavily modified version marketed as *"AskTurret Pro"* ❌
- A compatible reimplementation called *"AskTurret Clone"* ❌

If you maintain a fork or alternative implementation, clearly label it as such and avoid using the AskTurret name in the title.

## Enforcement Approach

AskTurret's trademark enforcement is **pragmatic and community-friendly**:

### Good-Faith Community Use

We do not police good-faith community contributions, tutorials, or small projects. If you're building something for the community, we trust you to use the name responsibly.

### Enforcement Triggers

We will enforce the trademark only when:

1. **Consumer confusion is likely** — A name or product misleads people into thinking it's an official AskTurret offering.
2. **Impersonation occurs** — Someone falsely claims to represent or speak for AskTurret.
3. **Commercial exploitation** — A company profits from AskTurret's brand without permission or contribution to the project.
4. **Dilution or tarnishing** — The name is used in a way that harms the AskTurret brand's reputation.

### How to Resolve Issues

If we believe your use violates this policy, we will:

1. **Contact you privately** to explain the concern and discuss resolution.
2. **Propose a remedy** — typically a name change or disclaimer.
3. **Escalate if necessary** — only if informal resolution fails.

We believe in solving disputes fairly and assume good faith.

## Trademark Registration

AskTurret intends to register the "AskTurret" name and logo as trademarks in key jurisdictions. This is separate from and complementary to the Apache 2.0 open-source license.

**The distinction:**

- **Apache 2.0 license:** Covers the *source code*. You can freely use, modify, and distribute the code under the license terms.
- **Trademark:** Covers the *name and identity*. You cannot claim to be AskTurret or use the name in a way that creates confusion.

Both work together: you have all the code freedoms, but you can't impersonate the project.

## FAQ

### Q: Can I use "AskTurret" in my GitHub project name?

**A:** If the project is an adapter, extension, or tool for AskTurret, yes. Examples:
- *"koa-askturret"* ✅
- *"askturret-policy-engine"* ✅
- *"askturret-examples"* ✅

If the project is not related to AskTurret or might be confused with it, please use a different name.

### Q: Can I create a YouTube channel called "AskTurret Tutorials"?

**A:** Yes, as long as it's clear that you're an independent community educator, not an official AskTurret channel. Include a disclaimer like: *"Independent tutorials for AskTurret MCP, not affiliated with the official AskTurret project."*

### Q: Can I charge money for my AskTurret adapter or tutorial?

**A:** Yes. You can build commercial products with AskTurret (that's the point of the open-source license). You just can't use the "AskTurret" name in a way that creates confusion about official vs. community products.

Example:
- *"Building MCP Tools with AskTurret: Professional Course"* ✅
- *"The AskTurret Pro Course"* ❌

### Q: Can I fork AskTurret and maintain my own version?

**A:** Yes, under the Apache 2.0 license. You must:
- Use a **different name** (not "AskTurret")
- Include a clear disclaimer that it's a fork and not affiliated with the official project
- Retain all copyright and license notices

Example: *"MCP-Layer: A fork of AskTurret focusing on minimal dependencies"*

### Q: What if I disagree with this policy?

**A:** We're open to feedback. Reach out to [trademark@askturret.com](mailto:trademark@askturret.com) with your concerns. We want a policy that's fair and supports the community.
